# c18-1171-proof

## Execution Notes

Authority: [#1171](https://github.com/TechsioCZ/ontos/issues/1171). Follow [foundation](c18-00-foundation.plan.md). Own public schema adaptations and private proof verification services/tests. Shared contracts may contain schemas, descriptors and pure validation; owner services and stored proof state stay inside Promotion.

Each application retains Promotion, optional Voucher and application identity, activation, configured effect, actual benefit, currency, exact target, basis mode/amount/source and exact allocations. Binding preserves Tenant, Selling Legal Entity, Channel, Market, actual Purchasing Subject or Guest Purchase Context, trusted time, exact candidate and every original occurrence with Catalog Selection/Quantity/Unit. Preserve independent identities for equal-valued occurrences.

Merchandise Monetary Proof is the complete Current merchandise part of this one application-set contract, including proven absence. It is not a new business resource or second application. Shipping may be unresolved when this part is complete. Later shipping evidence binds that same monetary result, publication, selected Delivery-owned offer/method, threshold outcome, separate ranking and Currentness. A monetary-only proof cannot claim complete Shipping or payable.

Owner completeness must cover every fact capable of changing the exact decision, including newly matching insertions, removals, lifecycle, Voucher changes and cart/free-shipping policies. Bind evidence to its predicate or a safely broader invalidating owner scope; returned rows or a winner revision alone do not prove completeness. Protect prospective joins from material changes between stages. Proof replay verifies original identities rather than minting another application. Historical evidence remains explainable after retirement and policy changes without claiming Current eligibility.

Preserve publication provenance separately from exact benefits. A required GROSS published source cannot be satisfied by Pricing NET, pre-round sums, UI numbers or caller-asserted basis. No minimum means absent criterion, not an implicit threshold. Owner-local doubles may model valid external evidence, but their use must be labeled as owner acceptance, not production integration.

## Constraints

No changes to old Pricing single-application/PRE_TAX contracts, no new evaluation/proof HTTP API or consumer client adoption under this issue, no GROSS producer, Delivery identity design or mandatory dependency for unrelated application compositions. Current and Accepted are separate claims. Follow foundation constraints.
