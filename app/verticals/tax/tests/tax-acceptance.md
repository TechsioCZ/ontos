# TAX owner acceptance (#961-#963) and the activation gate (#964)

The machine-checked source of truth for #961-#963 is `tests/unit/tax-acceptance-ledger.ts`. Its test checks that every coverage key is mapped, every named test exists verbatim, and that no gated row runs a test. This page explains the evidence classes and lists the #964 activation items.

## D6 evidence classes

| Class                | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `OWNER_PERSISTED`    | Real TAX over Postgres: Tax Rules, Seller VAT Regime Declarations and finals persisted through the owner services, read through the public read and Action handlers where the row says so. |

Real TAX covers rules, declarations, finals and the Accepted Tax Terms derivation. Foreign owners are contract-conforming test doubles, listed with their owner contract in `TAX_FOREIGN_OWNER_DOUBLES` (`tests/unit/tax-evaluation-fixtures.ts`): Catalog `catalogEntry`, the provisional declared-source `eligibilityHandoff`, Pricing `pricingLine`, Delivery `places` and `shippingCharge`, the Selling Legal Entity place `sellerPlace`, Order `purchaseBindingInput`, and Billing `acceptedTaxTermsInput`. Every result built from them is labelled `CALLER_SUPPLIED_UNVERIFIED`.

## Controlled doubles and limits

A green suite is TAX owner acceptance only (`TAX_OWNER_ACCEPTANCE_ONLY`). It is not Pricing→Tax (#892), not Catalog→Tax (Catalog publishes no Tax-purpose contract yet), not Checkout, Order or Billing E2E, not production configuration and not cutover. No gated row is reported as passed.

#893 is the Pricing external-source route (`status:now`, step 14). It is not a TAX route, TAX does not depend on it, and Launch has no external seller route.

### Concrete production blockers

The production handoff review found the following gaps behind these boundaries. A new owner regression or historical-read repair does not resolve them.

- Core has Legal Entity identity and an authorized name/status Read, but no public versioned seller DIČ, registered-address and register-details producer with governed revise/CAS/recovery. The activation and Billing consumers must use the exact identity revision; Q10 also covers scheduled future payers.
- Catalog's consumer-purpose vocabulary excludes TAX. Existing exact-selection and attribute persistence does not identify a governed `TAX_CATEGORY` or supply-meaning source. A real TAX-purpose Read must publish usable facts for exact configuration, package and Set composition. Neither a category label nor the provisional `eligibilityHandoff` proves that source.
- Pricing publishes real NET values, but its TAX handoff is a schema without the required production producer and immutable TAX monetary result/revision Read. #892 remains `status:park`. No Delivery/Shipping owner supplies the customer charge, and the available address-resolution external ports still fail unavailable where their owners are required. The real payable composer remains absent.
- New finalization accepts caller-provided foreign facts and labels them `CALLER_SUPPLIED_UNVERIFIED`. Actual owner retrieval/verification and source-currentness checks must precede any production commitment. Caller authentication, `CURRENT`, completeness strings and evidence hashes do not grant authority over those facts. Historical finals must retain their original trust label and recover without new live-source reads.
- The checkout, Order, Bundle/Attempt, Billing and Aftercare owner implementations needed by the complete purchase path are absent. #329-#331 remain `status:park`; TAX persistence does not supply their durable submission/T anchor, Order outcome reconciliation, predecessor closure, customer reconfirmation, document acceptance or refund entitlement.
- No actual adviser answer was found in the inspected authorities. Billing/later-event issuance, non-payer document wording, partial credit and the Q11.2 blocked-case matrix remain blocked on that input and the resulting approved owner work. Order T is not a universal later-event tax time.
- TAX owner-local Privacy coverage and declaration import do not establish downstream copy obligations or customer migration reconciliation. The Privacy collector still lacks an approved subject-to-TAX scope authority receipt. Seller-scoped `PARTIAL` and owner outages must survive aggregation; a local database test is not a customer-format rehearsal or cutover.
- Technical readiness and generic module activation do not enforce the #964 business evidence. Production needs independently verified seller/environment/release evidence, rule coverage, permissions/audit, legal/PO conclusions, operations and deactivation/recovery. The assessor remains `evidenceLabel: NON_PRODUCTION`, `activationClaim: NONE`, `commerceReadinessClaim: NONE`; filling reference strings cannot activate a scope.
- Rehearsal #903/#879, production acceptance #904/#880 and cutover #905/#881 remain `status:park`. Their execution approval and target-specific deployment, smoke, recovery and customer-path evidence are separate from TAX owner acceptance.
