# Business / Sales Value Brief

Date: 2026-06-04
Audience: business development, sales, leadership, customer-facing stakeholders

## One-Sentence Positioning

OntOS is a modular ERP foundation for property, finance, accounting, reporting, and operational data that helps companies replace fragmented spreadsheets, paper, and disconnected tools with one auditable operational system.

## The Customer Problem

Many property and operational businesses run important work across:

- spreadsheets
- paper records
- external accounting tools
- reservation systems
- email and chat
- disconnected document storage
- manual reporting

This creates predictable business problems:

- data is duplicated or inconsistent
- people do not know which record is current
- billing and accounting handoff are slow
- ownership, occupancy, contracts, payments, documents, and tasks are hard to connect
- management reports require manual consolidation
- permissions and audit evidence are weak
- handover, acceptance, and compliance evidence are hard to prove

OntOS is designed to reduce that fragmentation.

## What OntOS Delivers

For V1, OntOS targets six business areas:

1. **Property management**
   - properties, spaces/units, capacity, occupancy, contracts, operational tasks, client/service-user records, and related import/export.

2. **Accounting support**
   - issued and received invoices, numbering series, basic accounting agendas, CZK/VAT support, bank statement or data-file import, accounting export, receivables, and payables.

3. **Finance**
   - cash-flow views, payments, budgets, cost/revenue items, selected approvals, and management views by center/project where needed.

4. **Reporting and data**
   - dashboards, exports, consolidated views, data sources, and logical links between modules.

5. **Shared ERP functions**
   - users, roles, permissions, audit records, access security, backup/recovery procedures, and Czech documentation or UI support as required.

6. **Migration and interfaces**
   - import of historical data from spreadsheets, external systems, and digitized paper records, plus selected import/export interfaces.

## Business Benefits

### 1. One Operational Source Of Truth

OntOS gives the company a shared operational record for properties, units, contracts, tasks, documents, billing, payments, and reporting. Teams spend less time reconciling spreadsheets and more time acting on current data.

### 2. Faster Billing And Accounting Handoff

By connecting property/rental operations to invoices, receivables, payables, accounting documents, and export evidence, OntOS reduces manual handoff work and lowers the risk of missing or inconsistent billing data.

### 3. Better Management Visibility

Reporting and data modules provide dashboards and exports across ERP data. Management can see operational status, financial status, occupancy, receivables, costs, and export readiness without rebuilding reports manually each time.

### 4. Stronger Control And Accountability

Roles, permissions, audit logs, and action history make it clearer who did what, when, and under which context. This matters for internal control, customer acceptance, security, and future compliance.

### 5. Cleaner Data Migration And Handover

The project includes migration planning, import/export samples, validation records, and acceptance evidence. This reduces go-live risk and makes handover more defensible.

### 6. Modular Growth Instead Of One-Off Custom Software

OntOS is built around business modules. The first V1 modules solve the current ERP need, but the same Core can support future modules without rebuilding identity, permissions, audit, data links, reporting, and migration rules from scratch.

## Why The Modular Approach Matters

The customer needs a useful ERP by the end of 2026, but the exact details inside each module should be refined through customer use-case discovery.

The modular approach allows:

- fixed module areas for delivery planning
- flexible feature details inside each module
- faster customer feedback
- safer phased delivery
- future module expansion
- less risk of building a rigid system that misses real workflows

In sales language: OntOS is not just a one-off implementation. It is a reusable ERP platform foundation with a first concrete delivery in property and finance operations.

## What Makes OntOS Different

OntOS is designed around business traceability from the start:

- each important operation runs through a registered action
- important changes are audited
- asynchronous work is handled through outbox/background processing
- permissions are central, not scattered across module code
- business records can reference each other across modules
- reports and exports are treated as first-class delivery evidence

This gives OntOS a stronger foundation than an ad hoc custom app assembled screen by screen.

## Customer-Facing Value Message

OntOS helps the customer move from fragmented operational administration to a controlled ERP environment where property data, accounting handoff, finance views, documents, permissions, migration, and reporting are connected and auditable.

The goal is not to replace every specialist tool immediately. The goal is to create the operational backbone: the place where the company can manage work, connect records, control access, export data, and prove what was delivered.

## Sales Conversation Starters

Use these questions to uncover value:

1. Which operational records are currently spread across spreadsheets, paper, and external systems?
2. Where does billing or accounting handoff currently take the most manual effort?
3. Which reports does management rebuild manually today?
4. Which data must be trusted at handover or audit time?
5. Where do permissions or responsibilities create operational risk?
6. Which historical data must be migrated to make the new system useful?
7. Which workflows must work by the end of 2026 for the project to be considered successful?
